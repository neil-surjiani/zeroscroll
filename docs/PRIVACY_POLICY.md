# ZeroScroll Privacy Policy

*Last Updated: September 30, 2026*

ZeroScroll is developed by Apex Ventures ("we", "us", or "our") with a foundational commitment to user privacy, focus, and digital wellbeing. ZeroScroll is an Android application designed to provide a mindful, distraction-free environment for accessing your Direct Messages and Notifications/Activity while eliminating algorithmic feeds, exploration reels, and endless scrolling.

This Privacy Policy explains how ZeroScroll handles data, our strict zero-telemetry architecture, and our commitment to on-device data sovereignty.

---

## 1. Core Principles

- **No Remote Backend**: ZeroScroll has no backend servers, no user accounts with Apex Ventures, and no cloud databases.
- **Strictly On-Device Processing**: All credentials, session cookies, direct messages, and notification alerts are processed and stored exclusively inside your device's secure sandbox.
- **Zero Third-Party Telemetry**: ZeroScroll contains no third-party tracking libraries, advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics, or crash reporters.
- **Independent Application**: ZeroScroll is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or associated with Meta Platforms, Inc. or Instagram.

---

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect or Transmit

ZeroScroll's software architecture enforces strict network isolation outside the embedded web session:

- **No Credentials or Passwords**: When you sign in to your social media account, authentication happens entirely within an isolated, sandboxed WebView directly on the official login webpage. ZeroScroll never captures, intercepts, stores, or transmits your username, password, or two-factor authentication tokens.
- **No Direct Messages or Chat Content**: Your conversations, multimedia attachments, voice notes, and message histories remain solely in the web browser cache on your local device.
- **No Notification or Alert Content**: Notification summaries captured by the optional notification listener service are stored in a local SQLite database on your device and are never transmitted to any external server.
- **No Biometric Data**: When you enable Biometric App Lock, authentication is handled natively by the Android OS (`BiometricPrompt` / Keyguard API). Biometric templates (fingerprint, facial data) never leave Android's secure hardware enclave (TEE) and are never accessible to ZeroScroll.
- **No Device Identifiers or Telemetry**: We do not collect Advertising IDs, IMEI, MAC addresses, hardware serials, location data, or contact lists.

---

## 3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

ZeroScroll stores minimal operational data strictly within Android's private internal storage sandbox (`/data/data/com.apexventures.zeroscroll/`):

1. **WebView Session & Cookies**:
   - Authentication cookies and website cache generated by the embedded WebView to maintain your login session.
   - Stored in the native WebView sandbox and explicitly excluded from Android cloud backups and external extraction.

2. **Captured Notification Records (Optional)**:
   - If the Android Notification Listener Service is authorized by you, direct message and activity notification summaries (title, body, timestamp, sender) are stored locally in an encrypted/sandboxed SQLite database.
   - Subject to automatic 7-day pruning (configurable). You can disable capture or delete all stored records at any time.

3. **Digital Wellbeing Metrics & Preferences**:
   - Daily active foreground screen time and reel count metrics to enforce your user-defined budget and daily limits.
   - App preferences (dark/light theme, lock timeouts, screenshot protection, focus schedules).
   - Stored locally via Drift (SQLite) and `SharedPreferences`.

4. **Cached Remote Route Configuration**:
   - Centralized rule definitions (allowed route patterns, CSS content blockers) fetched via HTTPS to keep route guards functioning smoothly.

---

## 4. Permitted Network Communications

ZeroScroll makes no outgoing network requests other than:

1. **Embedded Web View**: Direct HTTPS traffic between your device and the official Instagram web domain (`https://www.instagram.com`) for rendering allowed message and activity interfaces.
2. **Remote Route Configuration (HTTPS GET)**:
   - Periodic download (every 12 hours) of a static, read-only JSON configuration containing updated CSS selectors and URL patterns.
   - Transmits no user identifiers, IP logging headers, or device tokens.
3. **APK Update Checks (Sideload Flavor Only)**:
   - In sideloaded builds, periodic HTTPS GET requests to fetch `version.json` for new release availability.

ZeroScroll disables all cleartext (HTTP) traffic and enforces modern Android Network Security Config.

---

## 5. Security & Protection Features

To protect your privacy on-device, ZeroScroll includes:

- **Biometric / PIN App Lock**: Require fingerprint or device PIN on launch and after a background timeout (immediate, 1 minute, 5 minutes).
- **FLAG_SECURE Screen Shielding**: Optional toggle to block screenshots, screen recording, and obscure the app preview in the Android Recent Apps switcher.
- **Backup & Extraction Exclusion**: Both `android:allowBackup="false"` and Android 12+ `dataExtractionRules` are configured to prevent transfer of session cookies or notification databases to cloud backups or external cables.
- **Zero Log Leaks**: All debug and diagnostic logging is compiled out in release builds.

---

## 6. User Control & Data Erasure

You maintain complete ownership of all data stored on your device:

- **Clear Captured Notifications**: Settings > Security > Privacy & Data Security > "Clear captured notifications" instantly purges all stored notification history.
- **Log Out and Wipe All Data**: Settings > Security > Privacy & Data Security > "Log out and wipe data" clears all WebView cookies, cache, local databases, shared preferences, and resets ZeroScroll to factory clean state.
- **System Uninstall**: Uninstalling ZeroScroll deletes all sandboxed storage, databases, and cached data immediately.
